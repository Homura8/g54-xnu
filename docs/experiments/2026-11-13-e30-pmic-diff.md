# E30: one power-key press starts a ~79 s MT6363 countdown to a cold reset (no XNU needed)

| Field | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| UTC date | 2026-11-13, flashed and rebooted 10:03:41 |
| Image | `out/g4/e30-pmicdiff-boot.img`, SHA-256 `c195173420b7d888240aa755903e8658094799c619692a616f9db165dce049c5`; XNU payload as E29 (K4); probe `out/g1-pmic` (`608fee9a…aaf`): E29's plus `-D PMIC_DIFF` |
| Method | Read-only through SWINF 2: snapshot MT6363 `0x000–0xfff`, mask registers that change in 2 s without a press, wait for one power press/release, diff, then stay **in the probe** for 6 min re-diffing every 30 s |
| Observation (owner photos, USB connected) | `UNREADABLE=0000`, `TOP=1E A0E=04 90F=09`, noise `N=000`. Press: `PRESSED=1 RELEASED=1`. **Changed by the press (N=7):** `0A6:1F>1B`, `42B:0C>03`, `91B:00>09`, `91D:00>09`, `A15:00>F0`, `A16:00>33`, `A17:00>01`. After 30 s: `0A6:1B>1F`, `A15:F0>91`, `A16:33>C1`, `A17:01>00`. **About 1 min 20 s after the press the phone went dark and rebooted** (Motorola unlocked-bootloader screen, then a normal boot), still inside the probe |
| Decode | `A17:A16:A15` is a **down-counter in ms**: 0x0133F0 = 78 832 right after the press, 0x00C191 = 49 553 ~30 s later; it reaches zero at ≈79 s, when the phone reset. `0x91B`/`0x91D` = PSC interrupt status (stock `mtk-spmi-pmic.ko` `mt6363_ints`: group PSC, hwirq base 0x30, enable 0x90f/0x912 with set/clr at +1/+2, status 0x91b/0x91c); bits 0 and 3 = hwirq 0x30 (power press) and 0x33 (`mtk-pmic-keys` second IRQ, release). `0x90F=09` means LK left exactly those two enabled |
| Result | **The press-linked reset is a PMIC-side timeout, independent of XNU/iOS.** The stock key IRQ handlers write nothing to the PMIC; the stock PMIC IRQ handler clears each status register (write the value back) and **always ends with `0x41b = 1`**, which we never do |
| Previous log | The LASTLOG page after this reset showed the previous iOS boot's tail mostly readable with scattered bit errors (power was cut briefly) |
