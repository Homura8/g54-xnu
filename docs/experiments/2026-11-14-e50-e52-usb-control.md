# E50–E52: USB control: screenshots, input, hands-free reflash (patch 0210)

**Result: PASS (E52, kernel K21).** Over the USB console the host can now take
screenshots, inject touches and keys, and reboot the phone into Fastboot; a
complete reflash loop needs no buttons.

| Run | Kernel (CI run) | Observation | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E50** | K19 (43606897225) | Port after 60 s; `ping` 0.15 s round trip; `status`, unknown-command help, live tail (no replay) work. `shot low` → panic `kmem_alloc(…, 10387456, 0x100021) failed with 3 @vm_kern.c:178` → Fastboot | Mapping the whole 10 MB scanout with `ml_io_map` exceeds the 8 MB `io_submap` |

| **E52** | K21 (43610154079, patchset `e1e10aed…`): scanout mapped once with `ml_io_map_unmappable` | Everything below works | **PASS** |
